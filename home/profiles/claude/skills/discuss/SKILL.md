# discuss

## Instructions

1. Interview the user about their plan one question at a time until every decision is resolved
1. Ask the questions first that other decisions depend on or that are hard to reverse
1. Give your recommended answer with each question
1. If the codebase can answer a question, explore it instead of asking
1. When the user doesn't know, record it as an open question and move on
1. Finish with the decisions made, the questions still open, the recommended approach and the next step
1. Don't implement anything
